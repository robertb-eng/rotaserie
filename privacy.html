Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

This explains what Rotaserie collects, why, and who can see it. In short: we collect what's needed to run a business's schedule, we don't sell it, and we don't run ads or third-party trackers.

What we collect

  • Account details: name and email. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), never in readable form. Two-step login (required for managers, optional for staff) also stores an encrypted key for your authenticator app and scrambled copies of your recovery codes. If you link your accounts at two locations of the same owner (after typing the other account's password and code), we keep which accounts are linked, so you can switch between them.
  • Scheduling information: shifts and positions, availability, time-off requests, shift trades and pickups, the reason picked when someone calls out of a shift ("sick", "family emergency" or "something came up", seen only by the managers who decide it), notes, and pay-related settings the business enters. If a manager sets up from a photo of their current schedule, the photo is sent once to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to read the names, days and times on it, together with the names of the people already on the team and the business's role names (so names are spelled the way the team list spells them); we don't keep the photo, and the manager checks what was read before anything is saved. People added this way by name only get no emails until a manager adds their address. When managers type shifts ("Type shifts" in the Schedule Builder), what they type is read on their own device; only the shifts they add are saved. To make typing shifts work better, lines it doesn't fully understand (or that a manager corrects or undoes right away) are sent to us keeping only the words Rotaserie already knows (days, times, numbers, roles, the business's shift words and the words it reads schedules with); every other word, people's names included, is replaced before it leaves the device, and again on our server, as are email addresses and phone numbers. We use them only to improve how typing is understood, keep them for 90 days, and delete them sooner with the business's data. A Business Admin can turn this off under Settings > Type to Build. We also count, with no text, how often the typing panel is used and how often it understands. Nicknames a manager chooses to remember for someone ("Manny" for Manuel) and the business's own shift words ("mid", "close") are kept with the business's data; a person's nicknames are deleted with their account. For staff under 18, their age group (14-15 or 16-17), so the schedule follows minors' work-hour rules; a birth date in an imported staff list is used only to work that out and isn't kept.
  • Business information: business name, address, settings, and optionally daily sales figures imported from a point-of-sale export.
  • Activity and security records: a log of schedule changes (who changed each shift, and when), when each person first signed in and last opened the app (we use the counts, never names, to see how well Rotaserie is working for a business), emails we sent, and failed login attempts and new business sign-ups with the device's IP address (to block password guessing and abuse). A sign-up waiting for its email to be confirmed (the name, email and scrambled password) is deleted after 24 hours and never copied off-site. Our host also keeps ordinary server logs. When a page or the app breaks, it sends us an error report holding only the page, the kind of error and the kind of device (web, iPhone or Android): no message, name or other personal data. We keep the daily counts for 14 days; the IP address is used only in memory, to limit repeated reports.
  • Support access: we can't see a business's schedules, staff or pay. When a Business Admin asks for help, they can let Rotaserie support see the business for 72 hours, read-only (nothing can be changed, and people's account pages stay private); while it's on, support may also download the business's data export to look into the problem. If a business is paused, we may download its data export for its owner when they ask. Every page support views, and every export, is recorded in the business's activity log, and a Business Admin can end support access at any time.
  • Hours worked: if a business turns on time tracking, the times staff clock in and out, and any correction a manager makes (with who made it and why). Business Admins can turn these into pay summaries; we don't collect Social Security numbers, home addresses or bank details for payroll.
  • Checklists and handover notes: if a business uses them, who ticked each item and when, and the short notes staff leave for the next shift. Both are deleted after 90 days.
  • Kudos and the monthly pulse check: thank-you notes staff send each other (deleted after 90 days; anyone can report a note, which hides it until a manager restores or removes it, and who reported it isn't kept), and answers to "How was work this month?", stored without who gave them (only a one-way code that stops answering twice, made with a key that isn't kept with the data); managers see only a finished month's average, from at least 5 answers. Answers are deleted after about a year.
  • Credentials and skills: if a business records them, the licenses, certificates or cards someone holds (for example a food handler card), their issue and expiry dates, an optional note such as a card number, and a skill level per job. We don't store copies of the documents themselves.
  • Tips, commission and bonuses: if a Business Admin records them, the amounts per day and person (seen only by Business Admins, for payroll). Rotaserie doesn't pay them out.
  • Pay rules: pay rates per job, and the night, weekend, split-shift and call-in pay rules a business sets (seen only by its Business Admins).
  • Requests to join: if you ask to join a business from its welcome page, the name, email, jobs and note you enter, the password you choose (kept only as a one-way hash, never as typed, and removed from the request once it's decided), and your device's IP address (only to limit repeated requests: erased from the request once it's decided, and from the count of requests after a day). Requests nobody decides are deleted after 30 days.
  • Sign-in links: if a manager makes you a sign-in link, only a fingerprint of it is kept (it works once, for 7 days). If you add your email on that link's page, it's kept with a confirmation link for up to 7 days, and becomes your login email only once you confirm it from that inbox.
  • Notifications: if you turn on push notifications, your browser's notification address (in the iPhone app, the phone's notification token from Apple).

How we use it

To show schedules, send reminders and notices, apply the business's rules, keep accounts secure, fix problems and improve the app. Managers of your business can see its staff list and schedule data; pay rates and labor costs are visible only to its Business Admins (owners and general managers). Other staff see what the app shows them, such as the schedule and who they work with. Data from one business is kept separate from every other business's.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in, protect forms, remember a device for two-step login when you ask us to, and remember your display choices (like dark mode). We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The Android app marks its own window (stored only in that window, not a cookie) so billing pages can be left out there. The iPhone app keeps a copy of your own upcoming shifts on your phone, so you can see them without a connection and in its widget; it is replaced each time you open the app and removed when you sign out. When a manager types shifts, their unsaved typing is kept in that browser (not a cookie, never sent to us until they add the shifts) so a call or a lost connection doesn't lose it; it is removed when they add the shifts, clear it or sign out, and after 14 days at most.

Who else handles it

  • Render hosts the app and its database.
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage) stores encrypted off-site backup copies: every copy from the last day, then one a day for two weeks and one a week for eight weeks, plus each business's two newest copies until the business is deleted; until that move is complete, GitHub does. They are scrambled before they leave our server and neither can read them.
  • Brevo delivers our emails, so it receives recipients' email addresses and message text.
  • Anthropic (Claude): only if a manager sets up from a photo of their schedule. It receives that photo once, with the names of the people already on the team and the business's role names, to read the names, days and times on it; we don't keep the photo.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments. A Business Admin enters card or bank details on Stripe's own pages, so they go to Stripe and never to Rotaserie. We keep the business's Stripe customer number, its plan and whether it's paid; Stripe receives the business's name and the Business Admin's email.
  • Calendar apps (Google, Apple, Outlook): only if you choose to add your shifts, using a private link.
  • Push services (your browser's, or Apple's for the iPhone app): only if you turn on notifications. They never receive the message itself: a browser gets an empty signal and then fetches the message from us; the iPhone app gets only a notice that you have an update, and you see it in Rotaserie when you open it.
  • Systems a business connects: if a Business Admin makes an API key or adds a webhook (Settings > API), the system they choose (for example their payroll or booking software) can read the team list (names, emails and jobs) and the schedule, and receives schedule and time-off events. It never receives pay information. That business decides which systems to connect and is responsible for them.

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only with these providers to run the service, if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep a business's data while it uses Rotaserie. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days and password reset links expire quickly. Nightly backups are kept for 30 days. Encrypted off-site copies are kept in a private archive that we replace with a fresh one at least once a year. When a business asks us to delete its data, or cancels its plan with the Cancel button and the days we keep it afterwards (shown on the Billing page) pass, we permanently delete it from the app, its local backups and our off-site archive, and anything left in older copies disappears as they age out. We keep only the business's name and billing record (what it paid and when) for our accounts.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Staff should start with their manager, who can change or remove records in the app (the logs of activity and schedule changes can't be edited, so they're kept while the business uses Rotaserie and deleted with its data); you can also email us. A business can ask us to delete all its data.

Deleting your account: anyone can delete their own account in the app (Account, then Delete my account; how-to at app.rotaserie.com/delete-account). Your name, email, password, two-step login, phone alerts, availability, calendar link, credentials, kudos and the reasons on your time-off requests and call-outs are deleted from your account at once, with the emails waiting to be sent to you and the business's list of emails sent to you. The records your employer must keep by law (hours worked, pay, tips and shift history) stay with your employer under "Former staff" and a number, and your managers are emailed which name and email that was (a copy of that email stays in the business's list of sent emails, so it can match its payroll records). What was already written about you keeps your name as it was written until the business's data is deleted: lines in the activity log (which can't be edited), and messages already sent to other people that mention you (for example an email about a shift trade with you). Backups age out as described above. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.

You can turn off push notifications in your browser at any time.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, forms are protected against forgery, and login attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so if you suspect a problem please tell us.

Children

Rotaserie is for workplace use and is not directed at children under 13. Businesses may schedule staff who are minors; the business is responsible for having the right to enter their information.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and email businesses about significant changes. Questions or requests: robert@rotaserie.com.
