The facts

What Rotaserie does, in plain words.

Rotaserie is scheduling for independent restaurants and cafés: build next week, send it to everyone's phone, and handle swaps, time off and call-outs, in English or Spanish.

What it does

  • Type to Build: type the week in plain words, in English or Spanish ("maria tue-fri 9-5"), and it becomes a schedule you check before saving.
  • Setup from a photo: take a photo of the schedule you use today; Rotaserie reads the names and shifts once, and you check them before anything is saved.
  • Staff join with a QR code and choose their own password. No app store needed: it works in any phone's browser and can sit on the home screen.
  • English or Spanish for each person: every screen and email, in the language each person picks.
  • Publishing tells each person what changed for them, and shows who hasn't seen the new schedule yet.
  • Quiet hours: new businesses start with no alerts from 10 pm to 7 am, except urgent changes to shifts starting soon.
  • "I can't make it": staff can say so from their phone; the manager sees it at once and can offer the shift to people who can work it.
  • Swaps, pickups, time off and availability, with the manager's approval where it's needed.
  • Washington rules checked as you build: meal breaks, rest between shifts, overtime, minors' hours and minimum wage. They're helpers, not legal advice.
  • Hours and labor cost for the owner, a pay summary to download, and an optional time clock.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't run payroll, process tips payouts or connect to a point-of-sale system.
  • It doesn't track staff by GPS.
  • It doesn't use AI to build your schedule. The one AI step is reading a photo at setup, if you choose it.
  • Store apps for iPhone and Android aren't out yet.

Price

  • One price per location, with unlimited staff and every feature included.
  • You can cancel any time from the app, and download all your data whenever you like.
