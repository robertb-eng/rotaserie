From typed lines to a published week.

The real app, recorded in real time with sample data.

    Type the week like a text.

    One line per person, “maya wed 5-11”. Rotaserie shows what it read, asks when something is missing, and adds the shifts in one tap.

What your staff see

Their schedule, on their phone.

Staff don't download anything. You add them, and they scan your QR code to put Rotaserie on their phone and sign in once. From then on they see their next shift, pick up open shifts, swap with a coworker and ask for time off, in English or Spanish.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Do I have to type the schedule?

No. Typing is the fastest way, but you can also drag shifts on the week, tap your usual shift times, copy last week, or keep a regular schedule that fills itself in.

Do my staff need to download an app?

No. Rotaserie runs in any phone's browser. Staff add it to their home screen and get phone alerts like any app.

Is it only for restaurants?

No. It was built at a restaurant, and it's ready for cafés, shops, salons, fitness studios and field crews, each with its own starting roles and shift times.

Does it replace my point of sale or time clock?

No. Keep clocking in where you do now. Rotaserie handles the schedule, swaps, time off and messages around it, and has its own time clock if you want one.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. Everyone chooses English or Spanish for their own screens, emails and phone alerts.
